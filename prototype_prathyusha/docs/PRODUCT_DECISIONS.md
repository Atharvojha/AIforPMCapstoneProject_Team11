## D-053 (proposed 2026-11-11, not built): Listing Lie Detector as the headline feature
**Why:** Q-032. Recommending and claim lookups in a fixed catalogue are easy to copy with a chatbot. What a chatbot cannot do is show receipts or say "nobody has tested this".
**What:** Student pastes a seller listing, shop pitch, or "my friend says…" text for ANY laptop. We split it into separate claims (spec, measured, experience), check each one: spec vs the listing's own numbers, battery/weight vs lab pages found live (Tavily, allowlisted), experience claims vs owner reviews when we have that laptop, else vs same-chip/same-line laptops we do have, labelled as such. Output: a red/amber/green line per claim with the exact source, plus "what to ask the seller".
**Reuses:** `claim_checker`, `research` live pipeline, `advisor.check_free_claim`, source labels.
**Not:** a chatbot answer with no sources; anything is shown as fact without a quote or link.

## D-053 update (2026-11-11): BUILT. Listing Lie Detector
Paste any listing, ad or pitch. A local model (llama3.1) splits it into claims by copying exact words (code throws away any quote that is not in the pasted text; instruction-like lines are removed before the model sees it; if the model is down, rule-based splitting takes over and the page says so). Code then decides every verdict: spec claims vs our listing data when the laptop is in the catalogue, else "only the seller's word"; the same listing contradicting itself (8 GB and 16 GB); battery hours vs Wh by arithmetic (rule-of-thumb watt lines, labelled); "good for gaming/video/coding" vs our needs rules (vendor links where a page states a number); experience claims vs owners of that laptop, else of that brand, else of laptops with dedicated graphics (always labelled "NOT this model"); praise without a number. Ends with "Ask the seller" questions built from what the listing leaves out. Optional online lookup (existing live research) is offered, never run automatically (search quota).
**Why this is the USP:** works for ANY laptop text, shows receipts, and says "only the seller's word" instead of guessing. A general chatbot gives fluent answers without receipts.
**Honest limits:** owner evidence exists for 14 + 15 laptops and their brands only; the watt thresholds (3.5 W / 6 W) are our rule of thumb; no answer-key evaluation yet (Q-033).
