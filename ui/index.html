Which reviews are about you?

Pick who you are and the laptops you are weighing. We read each laptop's reviews for you and show what applies to your use, what could be a dealbreaker, and where the evidence is thin or conflicting.

How we read the reviews
  • Applies: the reviewer used the laptop in a way that overlaps yours, on the same configuration as the listing.
  • Dealbreaker: a problem with something you care about, reported by two or more reviewers who apply to you.
  • Watch-out: one reviewer mentions a problem. Worth knowing, not enough to rule a laptop out.
  • We show the quotes behind every claim and never rank laptops. The choice is yours.
